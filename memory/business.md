# Business

## What Amer does
AI and n8n automation consultancy.

## Services offered
- n8n automation workflow builds for businesses — this is the one core/main service line — fully customized workflow: planning, building, credential/integration connections. Pricing: $750–$1,500 USD is the average; range spans $200 minimum (small workflow, e.g. a simple Gmail workflow) up to $5,000 for advanced builds. Any required monthly subscriptions (tools/APIs the workflow depends on) are billed separately, starting at $30/month.

## Positioning
- Target client profile: broad — any business except large companies/organizations. Example of a client he can take on: an e-commerce business doing ~400 orders/day.
- Industries: salons, clinics, real estate; strongest fit is customer support and e-commerce
- Client size: solo through mid-size businesses; excludes large companies/organizations
- What makes his offer different:
  - Full consultation, not just workflow delivery
  - Uses AI to plan and detect client problems (this agent is part of that — deeper client analysis aimed at raising KPIs toward ROI)
  - Fully custom builds, not off-the-shelf n8n templates
  - Ongoing relationship: stays with the client to fix bugs and add new features to existing workflows (e.g. adding GEO search to an existing social media workflow), not a one-off handoff

## Delivery model
- Typical engagement length: 2–3 weeks (pre-agent baseline; may compress with this agent's help)
- Standard phases: 2 discovery/scoping meetings with client → lands full technical workload/scope → build → deliver → optional enhancements if client wants them
- Pricing model: value-based, fixed price per project (not hourly). Deliberately sells value/outcome, not the product itself. Monthly subscription add-on (from $30/mo) for ongoing tool/API costs.
- Internal hourly rate: not used — Amer explicitly rejects hourly costing. Project hours vary too much (e.g. a project planned for 20h can run to 50h if problems surface, or finish faster than expected), so pricing is value-based per project, not hour-based.
- Target margin: 70–80% net profit margin on a project, then split roughly in half with a technical partner who helps deliver the work (so Amer's effective net share is roughly 35–40%). Partner may later be folded into a separate agent once this one is built out.
