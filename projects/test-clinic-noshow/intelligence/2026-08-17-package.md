## 1. Executive Summary
Clear Skin is a 2-dermatologist clinic with phone-only booking, no CRM, and a
receptionist who manually calls every patient to remind/confirm appointments. The
owner's stated priorities are (1) reduce no-shows, which she says cost her money, and
(2) free her receptionist from being "stuck on the phone all day." Recommended
solution: an automated appointment reminder/confirmation flow plus a structured
booking intake, both over WhatsApp (matches the phone-based channel patients already
use). Value: eliminates ~30 min/day of manual reminder-calling, and addresses an
estimated **$31,200/year in no-show-related lost revenue** (ceiling figure — see §19
for why an achieved-reduction number isn't claimed). **Overall confidence: Medium-High**
— most facts are the client's own words from meeting notes (OBSERVED); the no-show
rate is OBSERVED (estimated) rather than (measured), since it's her own guess, not a
tracked figure — see §12/§27.

## 22. Alternative Solutions
- **Do nothing but add a shared digital checklist** — cheaper, but doesn't remove the
  manual calling bottleneck (§10) or fix double-booking.
- **Full practice-management SaaS** (generic booking/CRM platform) — solves booking and
  reminders but is more platform and cost than a 2-provider clinic needs; overkill
  relative to the actual ask.
- Recommended solution below sits between these: purpose-built, no bloat.

## 23. Recommended Solution
Custom n8n-based WhatsApp reminder + structured booking intake (§14), reusing a
channel patients already use by phone today. Matches Amer's existing tooling stack
(Meta/WhatsApp channels) without introducing a full CRM the clinic doesn't need yet.

## 24. Recommended Priorities

   the ranking.
2. Receptionist time relief (booking intake) — client-stated second priority.
3. No-show waitlist auto-fill — profit-positive but not client-requested, offered

Risk (data-privacy question, §21) doesn't reorder priority here but must be resolved
