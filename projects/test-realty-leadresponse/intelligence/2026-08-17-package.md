## 1. Executive Summary
Falcon Realty is a 3-agent brokerage with no CRM and no dedicated reception — leads
land in a shared inbox and a WhatsApp group, and go unanswered for hours while agents
are out showing properties. The client (measured, from her own timestamp review):
average first-reply time is **9+ hours** across 41 leads last month. She believes this
is costing them deals, though no conversion data exists to confirm it. Recommended
solution: automated instant lead acknowledgment + routing, so every lead gets a fast
first response regardless of who's out. Value is stated in **time/effort terms only**
— she explicitly declined to discuss commission or deal value, so no dollar ROI is
computed (see §18/§19). **Overall confidence: Medium** — the response-time baseline is
a solid first-party measurement, but the core claim that slow response is costing
deals is her stated belief, not a confirmed fact (see §9).

## 22. Alternative Solutions
- **Assign one agent as dedicated inbox-watcher on rotation** — cheaper (no build cost)
  but doesn't scale past showings colliding for all 3 agents, and depends on manual
  discipline exactly like the current broken process.
- **Full CRM platform** — would also solve tracking (closing the §12 UNKNOWN rows) but
  is more system and change-management than a 3-agent shop needs for the stated
  problem; overkill relative to the actual ask.
- Recommended solution below is narrower than a full CRM, matching the stated problem.

## 23. Recommended Solution
Automated instant acknowledgment + agent routing (§14), layered onto the existing
inbox/WhatsApp channels rather than replacing them with a new system the team would
have to adopt wholesale.

## 24. Recommended Priorities
1. Fast, guaranteed-coverage lead response — the client's sole stated priority; drives
   the ranking on its own, there being no second stated priority to weigh against it.
2. Lead outcome tracking — profit-relevant (closes the measurement gap in §12) but not
   requested, offered separately (§15) rather than bundled.
