## 1. Executive Summary
Nova Home Goods is a ~400-orders/day e-commerce store where support (150
inquiries/day, 6-hour average first response) is overwhelmed, and cart abandonment
sits at 68%. Both numbers come from the client's own tools, not guesses. Recommended
solution: automated order-status/FAQ self-service to cut response time and agent load,
plus an abandoned-cart recovery flow. Support labor costs **$240/day (measured)**;
total cart-abandonment exposure is **~$38,250/day — a raw exposure ceiling, not a
forecast of recoverable revenue (see §19)**. As in the prior two tests, no recovery
percentage is claimed for either number. **Overall confidence: Medium-High** — the
core metrics are measured, but two real gaps remain: no support-ticket-type
breakdown, and a client request ("something with our reviews") too vague to act on —
see §25/§26.
