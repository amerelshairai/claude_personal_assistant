Full detail: `projects/wisal-retreats/intelligence/2026-08-19-package.md`.
Preliminary viability read: `projects/wisal-retreats/analysis/2026-08-19-preliminary-viability.md`.

**The business:**
- Two current offerings: Women's Inner Harmony (Phuket, Oct 2026, $1,999) and
  Couples – Rekindle Emotions (Khulo, Georgia, Jul 2026, $3,000 — **currently
  sold out**).
- Positioning is emotional/spiritual, premium: "Escape the noise. Reconnect
  with your soul." Target buyer reads as affluent.
- Runs on Shopify for checkout; **no CRM, no live chat, no WhatsApp widget
  anywhere on the site** — everything inbound is handled manually by Mohammed
  personally, across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger.

**The stated ask (his own words, relayed by Amer):** an AI system that
detects high-value ("high-ticket") leads among inbound contacts, answers them,
contacts them, stores them in a database, and tracks their "desire"
(preferences/interests).

**The core pain:** he's one person fielding all inbound across three
platforms and can't keep up.

**Recommended direction (from the intelligence package):** a phased build —
Instagram first (his primary, most active channel, 4,885 followers), covering
auto-response + high-value lead scoring + a lead database, expanding to
WhatsApp and Messenger once the pilot proves itself. This phasing directly
serves the trust problem: a working pilot he can see, before asking him to
hand over everything at once.

**Real risks worth being upfront about, not hiding:**
- Automated messaging on Meta platforms carries real policy risk — Instagram
  is his primary marketing channel, not just a support line, so this needs to
  be built carefully within Meta's/WhatsApp's rules, not fast and loose.
  Naming this proactively (rather than waiting for him to worry about it) is
  itself a trust-building move given his stated caution.
- Storing personal preference/"desire" data from private conversations, for a
  wellness/mental-recovery-positioned brand, deserves a real conversation
  about what he's comfortable with the AI reading and storing.

## Open questions to raise

Straight from the intelligence package §26 — these are the ones that actually
block scoping, ranked by how much they change the build:

1. When you say "contact them," beyond replying to their message, do you also
   want the system to proactively reach out to leads who've gone quiet — or
   does "contact" just mean the initial response? *(This determines whether
   Phase 1 needs outbound follow-up logic at all.)*
2. How do you currently decide a lead is "high-value" or "high-ticket" — is it
   something they say, which retreat they ask about, or something else?
   *(Without this, lead-scoring can't be built — the single most blocking
   unknown.)*
3. Roughly how many messages do you get per week across WhatsApp, Instagram,
   and Messenger combined?
4. Do you already have a WhatsApp Business account/number set up, or would
   that need to be created?
5. Is there anyone besides you currently responding to these messages, or is
   it entirely you?
6. How comfortable are you with an AI reading and storing details from
   private conversations — anything you'd want it to never touch or store?
7. The Georgia retreat is currently sold out — worth adding a waitlist system
   to capture that overflow demand? *(Optional add-on, not core scope —
   gauge interest, don't scope it live.)*
